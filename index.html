About Our Cultural Association

Probaho Cultural Association is a community-driven Bengali cultural association based in Devanahalli, Bangalore. We come together to celebrate our heritage through festivals led by Durga Puja, our flagship celebration, along with Kali Puja, Saraswati Puja and Poila Boishakh — every one of them brought to life by the enthusiasm and contribution of our own community members.
